# Systematic Mapping Protocol: Quality of Experience and Privacy in Network Technologies

This document outlines the protocol for a systematic mapping study on the intersection of Quality of Experience (QoE) and privacy in network technologies.

## Research Questions
**Q1:** What privacy-preserving techniques are employed in quality of experience systems, and what are their implications for system design and implementation?

**Q2:** In which primary application domains are privacy-preserving QoE mechanisms applied, and how are these domains categorized?

**Q3:** What are the most common types of contributions (e.g., frameworks, methods, and models)?

**Q4:** What types of QoE data (e.g., subjective user feedback, objective network metrics) are commonly used in these studies?

**Q5:** What are the main challenges in implementing privacy-preserving QoE techniques, and how do these challenges vary across different application domains?

## Inclusion Criteria

*   Peer-reviewed articles published in the last 4 years (2020-2024)
*   Studies focusing on both QoE and privacy aspects
*   Articles written in English
*   Studies related to telecommunications and network services

## Exclusion Criteria

*   Studies not addressing both QoE and privacy
*   Papers focusing solely on Quality of Service (QoS) without QoE considerations
*   Non-peer-reviewed articles, grey literature
*   Studies not related to telecommunications or network services
*   Articles without full-text PDF access

## Study Selection Process

a. Initial screening: Review titles and abstracts
b. Full-text review of selected papers
c. Apply inclusion/exclusion criteria
d. Resolve disagreements through discussion among researchers

## Data Extraction

Extract the following information from each selected study:

*   Study type (e.g., empirical, theoretical, review)
*   Research focus (e.g., QoE measurement, privacy preservation, both)
*   QoE measurement approach (if applicable)
*   Privacy-preserving technique
*   Technologies employed
*   Application domain (e.g., mobile networks, IoT, cloud computing)
*   Network context (e.g., 4G, 5G, 6G)
*   Contribution type (e.g., method, tool, model, framework)
*   Evaluation method (e.g., experiment, simulation, case study)
*   Key findings and conclusions
*   Research gaps and future work suggestions


## Data Synthesis and Analysis

a. Categorize studies based on:

*   **Study type:** (empirical, theoretical, review)
*   **Research focus:** (QoE measurement, privacy preservation, both)
*   **Privacy-preserving techniques used**
*   **QoE measurement approaches**
*   **Technologies employed**
*   **Application domains:** (e.g., mobile networks, IoT, cloud computing)
*   **Network contexts:** (e.g., 4G, 5G, 6G)
*   **Contribution types:** (e.g., method, tool, model, framework)
*   **Evaluation methods:** (e.g., experiment, simulation, case study)

b. Analyze the distribution of studies across categories to identify research trends and gaps

**c. Identify trends and patterns in the research:**

*   Temporal trends in publication frequency
*   Emerging technologies and methodologies
*   Shifts in research focus over time

**d. Summarize key findings related to each research question:**

*   Synthesize results from multiple studies
*   Identify consensus and conflicting findings
*   Highlight novel approaches and their effectiveness

**e. Analyze the evolution of privacy-preserving techniques in QoE research:**

*   Track changes in privacy-preserving methods over time
*   Assess the effectiveness of different techniques
*   Identify emerging privacy concerns and corresponding solutions

**f. Compile and analyze key findings and conclusions from individual studies:**

*   Identify recurring themes and insights
*   Highlight significant contributions to the field

**g. Synthesize research gaps and future work suggestions:**

*   Aggregate future research directions proposed by authors
*   Identify common challenges and unexplored areas
*   Propose potential research opportunities based on gaps in current literature

**h. Conduct cross-analysis to identify relationships between:**

*   Privacy-preserving techniques and their impact on QoE
*   Network technologies and associated privacy challenges
*   Application domains and preferred QoE measurement approaches

**i. Develop visual representations of data:**

*   Create charts and graphs to illustrate trends and distributions
*   Use network diagrams to show relationships between concepts
*   Develop heat maps to highlight research intensity across categories

**j. Critically assess the collective body of research:**

*   Evaluate the overall state of the field
*   Identify strengths and weaknesses in current approaches
*   Discuss implications for practice and future research directions
